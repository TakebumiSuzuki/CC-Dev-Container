The headline tension

FY2025 was the firm's strongest financial year on record — yet it sits alongside a delivery-quality challenge that requires direct attention. The work ahead is to make delivery consistency match financial performance.

Firm-wide revenue reached $43.7M, a 13.0% increase over FY2024's $38.7M and the third consecutive year of double-digit top-line growth. Operating profit grew in step — $15.7M at a 35.8% margin — demonstrating that the firm is scaling without margin erosion. All five practices contributed to growth in a narrow 12–13.5% band, reflecting broad-based momentum rather than reliance on a single engine.

The headline, however, sits alongside a delivery quality challenge. On-time project completion fell to 54.8% in FY2025, down from 68.8% the prior year. Client satisfaction averaged 4.15/5.0 — marginally better than FY2024 — but six completed engagements scored below 3.5, and NPS remained essentially flat. Client retention recovered from its FY2024 trough (85.3%) to 89.3%, but has not yet returned to FY2023's 93.3% high.

Looking forward, the active project portfolio stands at $48.5M in contracted work across 58 engagements, with 85% of projects on track or ahead of schedule. The immediate priority for FY2026 is converting strong revenue momentum into stronger delivery consistency.

Strategic Priorities for FY2026

1

Restore project delivery consistency

The drop in on-time delivery from 68.8% to 54.8% is the most operationally urgent finding. The Triumph Automotive and Empire Retail cases both point to inadequate scope and sponsorship-change protocols. A firm-wide SOW standard for transformation engagements — with explicit scope gates and sponsor-change escalation clauses — addresses the root cause directly.

2

Convert detractors and improve NPS mix

With 45% of surveyed clients in the detractor band, the firm runs a retention risk not visible in the retention rate alone. A targeted account-recovery program for clients scoring below 50 — plus structural improvements in consultant continuity and turning recommendations into action plans — addresses the top two client-named themes.

3

Resolve the Technology & Digital capacity constraint

The combination of the highest external pipeline ($6.2M), highest revenue share (28%), and over-capacity utilization creates an imminent revenue ceiling. Approving the two Senior Consultant hires is the minimum action required to avoid lost pipeline conversion in the strongest-demand practice.

Conclusion

FY2025 demonstrated that Meridian can sustain double-digit revenue growth with stable margins across all five practices simultaneously. The $43.7M result and $15.7M operating profit are the firm's strongest on record, and the $48.5M backlog positions FY2026 for continued growth. The work ahead is to make delivery quality match financial performance — through stronger SOW standards, consultant-continuity commitments, and targeted capacity investment in Technology & Digital.

Anticipated Q&A

Revenue grew 13% but on-time delivery fell to 55% — are these connected?

Partially. Rapid growth can stretch delivery teams, but the specific root causes — inadequate scope gates (Triumph Automotive) and volatile client-side sponsorship (Empire Retail) — are process failures rather than pure capacity failures. The fix is structural, not just hiring.

Why is NPS flat despite strong retention and revenue growth?

Retention measures whether clients renew; NPS measures how they feel. A client can renew out of switching costs or inertia without becoming a promoter. The divergence signals that revenue metrics are healthy but relationship depth is not deepening at the same rate.

What is the risk on the six At Risk active projects?

The six at-risk projects represent ~10% of the active portfolio by count. Individual account reviews are recommended at the next QBR to assess remediation before any move toward partial delivery or early termination.

Is the Technology & Digital capacity constraint impacting FY2025 results, or is it a forward risk?

The over-capacity situation was flagged in April 2026 (Q1 2026), so it did not depress FY2025 results. It is a forward constraint: the $6.2M weighted pipeline in that practice cannot convert at full efficiency without additional headcount.

What was the total revenue impact of the two delivery-issue engagements?

Empire Retail invoiced $320K of a $500K contract — a $180K shortfall. Triumph Automotive's scope amendment was eventually processed, so its primary impact was a Q3 cash-flow timing gap rather than lost revenue. Combined revenue leakage is ~$180K against a firm total of $43.7M — immaterial to the top line, but meaningful as a leading indicator of delivery risk.
