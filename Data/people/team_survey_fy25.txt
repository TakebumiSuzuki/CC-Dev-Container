─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KEY THEMES — WHAT'S WORKING
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Quality of client work (avg score 81/100)
   Staff consistently rate the intellectual challenge and quality of MCG's
   engagements as a top reason for joining and staying. Comments: "The problems
   we solve are genuinely hard." "I learn more here in 6 months than I would
   in 2 years at a corporate role."

2. Peer quality (avg score 79/100)
   "I work with the smartest colleagues of my career." This theme appeared in
   37 of 104 open-ended responses. A strong signal for culture and talent density.

3. Leadership visibility (avg score 76/100)
   Partner accessibility improved noticeably vs. FY2024. Multiple respondents
   cited the re-launch of biweekly all-hands calls as a positive change.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KEY THEMES — AREAS OF CONCERN
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Career development clarity (avg score 58/100) — FLAGGED
   The most common open-ended concern: "I don't know what I need to do to get
   promoted." 28 respondents mentioned unclear promotion criteria, particularly
   at the Consultant → Senior Consultant and Manager → Senior Manager transitions.
   This is the largest driver of the YoY decline in the career growth score.
   Recommended action: Publish updated promotion rubrics by practice by Q1 2026.

2. Staffing and utilization pressure (avg score 55/100) — FLAGGED
   Technology & Digital practice respondents scored this category at 48/100,
   the lowest of any practice. Comments: "I've been above 90% utilization for
   four consecutive months." "There's no slack for learning or business development."
   Recommended action: Immediate hiring prioritization for Tech & Digital (2 Senior
   Consultants). Cap on individual utilization at 87% in Q1 2026 with monitoring.

3. Compensation competitiveness (avg score 61/100)
   Scores declined 4 points vs. FY2024. Eight respondents mentioned competing
   offers from Big Four or in-house strategy teams. The March 2025 compensation
   benchmarking showed MCG at 92nd percentile vs. regional boutiques but 78th
   percentile vs. national peers. A mid-cycle compensation adjustment was made
   in September 2025 for Senior Consultant and Manager grades.

4. Onboarding experience for new hires (avg score 54/100)
   Particularly flagged by employees in their first 12 months (n=22).
   "My onboarding was three days of HR paperwork and then I was staffed on a
   client." The new hire buddy program launched in September 2025 is too recent
   to show in this survey cycle.
