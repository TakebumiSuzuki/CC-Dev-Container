The year was not without friction, and this review presents it plainly: on-time delivery fell to 55%, the competitive win rate runs below benchmark, and the NPS mix skewed toward detractors. 3Retention

Client retention rebounded to 89%, recovering most of the ground lost in FY2024 (85%) though still below the FY2023 high of 93%. FY2025 non-renewals spread across pricing, competitive switching, and budget cuts — no systemic churn driver.

The recovery is encouraging — but two of the FY2025 losses were to direct competitors, a thread we pick up in Market Position.

VI

Conclusion

A strong, profitable year — and a clear, honest punch-list. The issues share roots, and already have owners and committed actions.

  • Profitable growth held — revenue $43.7M (+13% YoY) at a stable 35.8% operating margin.
  • Client signals mixed but improving — retention recovered to 89%; NPS mix remains detractor-heavy.
  • Engagement up, with two flags — overall 72/100, but career-growth clarity and work-life balance declined, concentrated in early-tenure and Technology & Digital staff.
  • Delivery is profitable but not predictable — 30.6% margin and 4.15 CSAT, undermined by 55% on-time delivery.
  • Demand isn't the constraint, conversion is — ~$48M weighted pipeline against a 29% competitive win rate, the clearest FY2026 growth lever.

10Anticipated Q&A

Revenue grew 13% — why reinvest now instead of banking the margin?

Because the margin is exactly what makes reinvestment affordable: at a stable 36% margin, funding promotion rubrics, targeted Tech & Digital hiring, and delivery-PM capacity protects the growth rate without diluting profitability.

Is the 55% on-time delivery rate a quality problem or a staffing problem?

Primarily staffing and schedule discipline, not quality — margins (30.6%) and CSAT (4.15) held up, so the work is good and profitable; it is finishing on schedule that slipped, which is what the delivery-capacity investment targets.

Our win rate trails the benchmark — is that a capability gap?

No — debriefs point to C-suite relationship and timing gaps, not capability; we lose late, above the formal process, to MBB/Big Four brand relationships, which is why the FY2026 plan invests in partner-led account relationships rather than new service lines.
