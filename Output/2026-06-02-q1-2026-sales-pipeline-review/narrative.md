## Competitive Dynamics

Pattern in recent losses: McKinsey took a $1.2M deal via a board/CEO relationship before formal scoping — 3rd "above-the-sponsor" loss in 18 months. Deloitte won a $900K energy RFP on sector accelerators despite our stronger proposal. Recent wins came from senior-in-the-work staffing, faster mobilization, and technology-agnostic positioning.

Pricing: MCG ~$295/hr — competitive vs. boutiques (~$220–280) and well under MBB (~$580–750). Don't compete on price; compete on breadth and track record. Mid-market squeezed from both ends — be selective on single-practice RFPs under $300K (Source: ./Data/pipeline/competitive_intel.txt).

## H2 2026 Revenue Bridge

At ~$10.3M/quarter, H2 target ~$20–21M. The $48M weighted pipeline (close May–Dec) gives >2× coverage. The build is healthy in structure — realization risk, not volume risk.

Key conversion variables:
- T&D win rate 25% → 40% adds ~$1.5M from current pipeline
- Verbal Commit ($27.4M, ~85% prob): execution risk, not sales risk
- Qualification ($2.3M): thin early-stage; replenish by Q3

## Recommendations

1. **C-suite relationship investment** — counter McKinsey's board-level entry; partners present at events for top 15 target accounts.
2. **T&D win-rate program** — structured win/loss debriefs on all H2 T&D deals; diagnose proposal-stage vs. sourcing-stage loss.
3. **NPS triage** — follow up the 16 detractors within 30 days; identify practice/AM concentration.
4. **AI advisory build** — plan 4–5 net-new hires in Q2 to capture H2 demand (~$3–4M).

## Conclusion

### Key Takeaways

- Q1 revenue $10.3M on target, but 5.7% YoY growth is a clear deceleration
- $48M weighted pipeline gives strong H2 coverage — risk is conversion, not volume
- T&D's 25% win rate is the single largest upside lever
- NPS –32 (vs. +52 in 2025) is an urgent client-health signal
- Losses to McKinsey/Deloitte/PwC are structural — answer with relationships and partnerships, not better proposals

### Anticipated Q&A

- *Why did growth slow if the pipeline looks healthy?* Q1 revenue came from 2025 wins; the pipeline closes in H2. Converting it at current rates should re-accelerate Q3–Q4.
- *Is the NPS drop real or a data artifact?* Small sample (28), but directional signal is real. Follow up all detractors within 30 days before concluding.
- *Should we be more selective given 40% win rate?* Yes — especially sub-$300K single-practice RFPs. Best win rates (50%) are multi-practice Ops/Strategy deals.
- *Can we build AI advisory without straining the team?* Net-new hires, not reallocation — utilization is already 76.2%, near top quartile.
- *How exposed is the Financial Services gap?* $6.0M weighted vs. firm-wide 4.6× coverage is light, against confirmed regulatory demand — a Q2 outbound priority.
