0Executive Summary

  • Revenue $43.7M, +12.9% YoY — above the 8.5% industry average growth rate
  • Operating margin 35.8%, stable vs. FY2024 (35.6%)

  • Weighted pipeline $48M; win rate 29% on tracked competitive deals, below the 33% industry average; primary loss driver is relationship coverage

VI

Part VI

Conclusion

Strong growth and margin stability in FY2025 — with a clear punch-list for FY2026.

11Summary

Strong growth and margin stability in FY2025. Two areas require focused ELT action in FY2026: on-time delivery discipline and BD relationship coverage.

12Key Takeaways

  • Revenue $43.7M (+12.9% YoY), margin 35.8% — growth without margin dilution
  • Client retention recovered to 89.3%; NPS and repeat-revenue metrics above industry average
  • People metrics broadly healthy; Technology & Digital capacity and junior career development clarity require near-term action
  • On-time delivery at 54.8% is the firm's sharpest operational gap — scope gate discipline is the primary fix
  • $48M weighted pipeline is strong; win rate improvement requires a relationship coverage strategy, not a pricing or quality fix

13Anticipated Q&A

Why did on-time delivery fall to 54.8%?

Two root causes: mid-engagement scope expansion without timely amendments (Triumph pattern) and client-side sponsor instability (Empire pattern). Fix is tighter SOW scope gates on transformation engagements.

If we're losing on relationships, not quality, what's the fix?

Deepen existing accounts before chasing new logos — 68% of revenue already comes from repeat clients, and relationship is the primary win predictor in our own data.
