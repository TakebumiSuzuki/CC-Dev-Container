## Conclusion

Strong growth and margin stability in FY2025. Two areas require focused ELT action in FY2026: on-time delivery discipline and BD relationship coverage.

### Key Takeaways

- Revenue $43.7M (+12.9% YoY), margin 35.8% — growth without margin dilution
- Client retention recovered to 89.3%; NPS and repeat-revenue metrics above industry average
- People metrics broadly healthy; Technology & Digital capacity and junior career development clarity require near-term action
- On-time delivery at 54.8% is the firm's sharpest operational gap — scope gate discipline is the primary fix
- $48M weighted pipeline is strong; win rate improvement requires a relationship coverage strategy, not a pricing or quality fix

### Anticipated Q&A

- *Why did on-time delivery fall to 54.8%?* Two root causes: mid-engagement scope expansion without timely amendments (Triumph pattern) and client-side sponsor instability (Empire pattern). Fix is tighter SOW scope gates on transformation engagements.
- *If we're losing on relationships, not quality, what's the fix?* Deepen existing accounts before chasing new logos — 68% of revenue already comes from repeat clients, and relationship is the primary win predictor in our own data.
